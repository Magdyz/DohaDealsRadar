What the app does

Community deals

People post the deals they find in Egypt — food, groceries, electronics, fashion, telecom and more.

Hot or cold votes

The community votes each deal up or down, so the best offers rise to the top and dead ones sink.

By governorate

Filter to Cairo, Giza, Alexandria or anywhere in Egypt, plus online-only deals.

No duplicates

The same link, photo or title is caught before it can be posted twice.

Arabic and English

Switch language with one tap; the whole app flips, including the layout.

Sign in with Google

One tap, no password. You get a random username, and we never store your email address.

Privacy

Browsing needs no account at all. There are no ads, no advertising IDs, no location tracking and no third-party analytics. You can download everything we hold about you, or delete your account for good, from inside the app. Full details in the privacy policy.

Support

Questions, problems or a deal that shouldn't be there? Use Feedback in the app menu and leave your email so we can reply.
